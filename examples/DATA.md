# Synthetic demonstration data

Both CSVs are generated by `generate_demo.py` with NumPy seed 52. They contain 180 monthly observations and four fictitious strategy series named Quality, Trend, Defensive and Value. These labels do not represent implementations of those real strategies. Returns are independent normal draws, not historical market observations. Do not use demo results as investment evidence.

`synthetic_excess.csv`: decimal monthly simple excess returns; no real fees are modeled (`--fee-status unknown`).

`synthetic_total.csv`: the same excess series plus 0.001 monthly cash return. `cash` provides the risk-free column. Pass `--return-type total --rf-column cash`; both examples should produce the same excess-return analysis within rounding tolerance.

Regenerate from the project root: `python examples/generate_demo.py`. The generated data and generator are covered by the project MIT license. No Bloomberg, publisher or course dataset is bundled.
